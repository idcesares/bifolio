# Bifolio Privacy Policy

Effective: on first public release

Bifolio is a reader for Markdown files on your device.

- **No data collection.** Bifolio does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting services.
- **No network access.** Bifolio does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- **Your files.** Bifolio only reads files and folders you choose to open. It never modifies them. It keeps, on your device only, a list of recently opened documents, your reading positions and your settings. You can remove documents from the recents list and revoke folder access in Settings.
- **Links.** When you open an external link, Bifolio asks for confirmation and hands it to your browser. What happens next is governed by that browser's policy.
- **Backups.** Bifolio opts out of Android backup and device transfer. Nothing it stores leaves your device that way.
